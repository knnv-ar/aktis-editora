*Áktis* (ἀκτίς) means "ray of light" in Ancient Greek.

Áktis Editora is a hybrid open access publisher based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. It publishes books, journals and texts on art, technology and aesthetics; the humanities and social sciences; creative writing; and technical and teaching materials.

## Open access

All publications can be read for free on the web and, where available, downloaded as PDF and EPUB. Each author chooses the license for their work. Print editions are sold separately.

## Collections

- **Áktis Investigación:** peer-reviewed academic books.
- **Áktis Ensayo:** author essays, outreach and cultural policy.
- **Áktis Creación:** poetry, fiction, drama and hypermedia works.
- **Áktis Manuales:** course notebooks and coding tutorials.
