Áktis Editora supports authors, university departments, research groups and institutions through the whole publishing process, from manuscript to book on the web, PDF, EPUB and print.

## Editing and proofreading

- Editorial reading and feedback on structure and style.
- Copyediting and proofreading (Spanish and English).
- Citation and bibliography formatting.

## Design and production

- Layout for on-screen reading, downloadable PDF and accessible EPUB 3.
- Print-ready PDF with bleed and cover with spine.
- Cover design.

## Enhanced publications

- Books and articles with audio, video and code.
- Hypermedia and interactive works embedded in the web edition.

## Registration and indexing

- ISBN and legal deposit in Argentina.
- Scholarly metadata (ORCID, Dublin Core, Google Scholar) and, later, DOI.
- Advice on Creative Commons licenses and copyright.

## Academic journals

- Design and online publication of journals with ISSN.
- Peer review workflow and publication by issues and articles.

## Print

- Print on demand or short runs, with online sales.
